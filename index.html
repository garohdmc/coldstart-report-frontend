    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What is a Cold Start Report?

    A pre-purchase research report for a specific used Harley-Davidson. It covers specs, recalls, known failure points, maintenance, a price check and an inspection checklist you can use at the dealership or private sale.

    How is this different from a vehicle history report?

    A history report tells you where a bike has been: titles, accidents, odometer records. Cold Start Report tells you what tends to go wrong on that model and year, and how to check for it before you pay. They answer different questions.

    How long does it take to get my report?

    A few minutes after payment. You can read it on screen, download it as a PDF, and it's emailed to you.

    Which bikes do you cover?

    Big Twin Harley-Davidsons from the Evolution era through Milwaukee-Eight: Touring, Softail, Dyna and FXR models, 1985 to current.

    Do I need the VIN?

    No. Year and model are enough. The VIN confirms the exact model year and lets you check it against what the seller told you.

    Is $29.99 a one-time charge?

    Yes. One payment, one report, no subscription.

    Are you affiliated with Harley-Davidson?

    No. Cold Start Report is independent. Harley-Davidson is a trademark of H-D U.S.A., LLC.
